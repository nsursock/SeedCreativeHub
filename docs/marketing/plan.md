## Executive Summary

- **Focus:** Seed Lebanon’s creative scene (Beirut density first) via a waitlist + founding-artist cohort + recurring Hub Nights, then grow into an MVP that makes discovery → connection → collaboration the default loop.
- **Outcomes:** 20–50 claimed/founding artist profiles before full launch; Hub Night sustained 8+ weeks; waitlist growth with high creator density; measurable “meaningful creative connections” once the product ships.
- **Timeline:** Phase 0 seed (weeks 1–12 🔶) → Phase 1 MVP launch marketing → Phase 2 depth (open calls, retention) → Phase 3 commerce messaging (later).
- **Contents:** Business strategy → product strategy → marketing strategy (goals, channels, content, roles, budget, contingencies).

## 1. Business Strategy

### Name
Creative Hub (working title). Candidates: Bayt, Makan, Beirut Creative Hub. 🔶 Final name required before paid creative / domain lock.

### Vision
A digital home for Lebanon’s creative communities — and the diaspora that still orbits them — where people discover work, discover people, find collaborators, and turn online connections into real projects and gatherings.

### Mission
Close the gap between fragmented Instagram/WhatsApp/gallery circuits and a cross-disciplinary place where Lebanese creatives are findable, contactable, and gathering IRL.

### Summary / boilerplate
Creative Hub is a connection platform for Lebanon’s makers — music, film, design, writing, visual art, performance, fashion, architecture, and more. Profiles, works, “looking for collaborator,” events, and Hub Nights turn discovery into collaboration. Not a portfolio site. Not a feed. Creative connection.

### Headquarters & locations
Operate for **Lebanon first** (Beirut density), then diaspora, then regional MENA/SWANA. No physical HQ required for Phase 0; marketing centers on Beirut scenes + remote diaspora channels. 🔶 Anchor venue / Hub Night partner TBD.

### Leadership
Founders own positioning, partnerships, and IRL. 🔶 Assign: brand lead, community/Hub Night lead, content/editorial lead, growth owner.

### SWOT

| | |
|---|---|
| **Strengths** | Clear local need; cross-discipline thesis; IRL Hub Night cold-start; Arabic-first dignity; specs already constrain scope |
| **Weaknesses** | No final name/voice; small team; payments/commerce deferred; platform fatigue in market |
| **Opportunities** | Fragmented discovery; diaspora bridge; venue/org partnerships; editorial collections; founding cohort lock-in |
| **Threats** | Instagram inertia; moderation/political sensitivity; connectivity/bandwidth; competitor copy of model; naming delay stalls brand |

## 2. Product Strategy

### Target market
Lebanese creative communities (Beirut-heavy), audiences/scene regulars, organizers, then diaspora creatives/patrons. Industry: local culture / creator discovery — not global “creator economy SaaS.”

### Competitive situational analysis

| Player | Strength | Weakness vs us |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram / TikTok | Habit, reach | No structured collab search; discipline silos; weak local “who’s making what” index |
| Behance | Portfolio craft | Global, not Lebanon-dense; weak IRL + events loop |
| WhatsApp / FB groups | Trust, speed | Unsearchable, ephemeral, closed |
| Resident Advisor / club listings | Event utility in niches | Not cross-discipline creative identity |
| Generic creator platforms | Features | No Lebanon density, Arabic-first, or Hub Night ritual |

**Not competing with:** RA for club listings; Behance for global portfolio scale; generic creator-economy SaaS.

### Customer analysis & buyer personas

1. **Creator (primary)** — Needs discoverability + collaborators without rebuilding presence from scratch.
2. **Collaborator-seeker** — Needs “looking for X in Beirut / remote” faster than DMs.
3. **Audience / scene regular** — Needs “what’s happening / who’s new.”
4. **Organizer / org** — Needs a concentrated creative audience for events and calls.
5. **Diaspora creative / patron (secondary)** — Needs a bridge home; monetization later.

### Product / service statement
We help Lebanese creatives (and their audiences) discover people and work across disciplines, start collaborations, and meet at Hub Nights — so the scene compounds instead of fragmenting.

### Unique selling proposition (USP)
**Local density + cross-discipline identity + structured connection + recurring IRL ritual.**  
🔶 Positioning voice still open:

| Option | Frame |
|---|---|
| **A — Utility** | The practical discovery / collaboration tool nobody built for Lebanon |
| **B — Solidarity** | Built by working artists for this scene, after what we lost |

### Price
MVP: free. Monetization deferred (memberships, commissions, ticketing in Phase 3). 🔶 Free vs sponsor vs eventual take-rate undecided.

### Place
Web product (Arabic-first, EN, FR as capacity). Distribution: landing/waitlist → claimed profiles → public shareable URLs → Hub Night QR/claim. No native apps in Phase 1.

### Promotion (unique offer)
- Founding artist invite / claim (recognition + early presence)
- Hub Night RSVP + coverage → profile claim within 7 days
- Editorial features / collections of real local work

### Market strategy
Win Beirut density before scale. Lead with IRL + easy embed/import of existing work. Prefer community depth and founding cohort over vanity ads. Expand to diaspora once the home scene is legible online.

## 3. Marketing Strategy

### Goals
1. Seed a trusted founding cohort before MVP.
2. Make Hub Night the recurring physical–digital flywheel.
3. Drive waitlist and claim rates among creators (not only audiences).
4. Establish distinctive brand voice (utility and/or solidarity — lock one primary).
5. Prove the north-star path: discover → connect → collaborate / attend.

### Objectives & key results (OKRs) — Phase 0 (🔶)

| Objective | Key results |
|---|---|
| Seed community | 20–50 founding / claimed profiles; ≥70% with bio + ≥3 works + disciplines |
| Sustain IRL | Hub Night running 8+ consecutive weeks (or equivalent cadence) |
| Capture demand | Waitlist with clear creator share; referral codes if enabled |
| Close the loop | % of Hub Night attendees who claim/update a profile within 7 days (track from first night) |

### Metrics & KPIs
- Waitlist signups (creator vs audience if tagged)
- Founding claim rate; profile completion
- Hub Night RSVP → attendance
- Event → profile claim within 7 days
- (Post-MVP) monthly active creators; works/week; collab posts + responses; meaningful connections/month; W8 retention of seeded artists

### Initiatives
1. **Founding cohort program** — invite/apply, claim links, lightweight onboarding help.
2. **Hub Night series** — partner venue, RSVP, coverage, claim CTA.
3. **Landing + waitlist** — Arabic-first value prop; email nurture.
4. **Editorial storytelling** — short features on founding artists / nights (not generic startup blog).
5. **Partnership outreach** — venues, universities, galleries, collectives, cultural orgs.
6. **Diaspora soft channel** — later Phase 0/1: stay-connected messaging without overselling.

### Tactics
- 1:1 founder outreach to seed artists
- In-person Hub Night + photo/video coverage with profile tags
- WhatsApp/IG stories and posts that link to claim/waitlist (habit channels → our URLs)
- Partner co-posts and flyer/QR at venues
- Email sequences: waitlist confirm → founding story → next Hub Night → claim reminder
- SEO-ready public profiles once MVP ships

### Channels
| Priority | Channel | Role |
|---|---|---|
| P0 | IRL Hub Nights + venues | Trust, density, claim conversion |
| P0 | Direct outreach (DM / WhatsApp) | Founding cohort |
| P0 | Instagram | Awareness + night coverage → link in bio / stories |
| P1 | Email | Nurture, RSVP, claim |
| P1 | Partner orgs / universities | Credible reach |
| P2 | TikTok / YouTube Shorts | Scene storytelling if capacity |
| P2 | Diaspora communities | Soft bridge once local proof exists |
| Later | Paid social / search | Only after message and name are locked |

### Content
- Landing hero + waitlist (AR / EN)
- Founding artist spotlights (short, visual, local)
- Hub Night recaps (people + works, not “we launched”)
- “Looking for collaborator” examples / social proof
- Guidelines for trust & safety (lightweight, clear)
- Event flyers / QR assets
- Avoid: generic “creator economy” hype; overclaiming marketplace features

### Roles & responsibilities (lean team 🔶)

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Founders / Product | Positioning lock, partnerships, budget, Hub Night strategy |
| Community / Hub Night lead | Venue, RSVP ops, night run-of-show, claim follow-up |
| Brand / content | Voice, landing copy, spotlights, night coverage edit |
| Growth / ops | Waitlist hygiene, email, tracking sheet, outreach CRM |
| Design (as available) | Visual system, flyers, landing polish |

### Partnerships
- Anchor Hub Night venue (open decision)
- Cultural orgs, galleries, collectives, universities
- Media / scene accounts for coverage (selective)
- Co-marketing with aligned nights/festivals — no pay-to-play that dilutes trust

### Tools (lightweight)
Landing site · waitlist/email (e.g. form + ESP) · spreadsheet / simple CRM for outreach · RSVP tool · shared drive for assets · analytics on landing · later: product analytics

### Process
Weekly marketing stand-up: pipeline (outreach → claim), next Hub Night, content calendar, blockers. Approve public voice against locked positioning (A or B). Specs change → update this plan + landing copy.

### Monitoring & controls
Founders approve: name, positioning, paid spend, partner deals, sensitive political framing. Content review before publish for moderation/safety alignment.

### Tracking & reporting
- After each Hub Night: attendance, claim %, notable connections
- Weekly: waitlist, outreach funnel, content shipped
- Monthly: OKR review; kill or double down on tactics

### Timeline (indicative)

| Window | Focus |
|---|---|
| Weeks 1–2 | Lock name + positioning 🔶; finalize landing; start founding outreach |
| Weeks 3–6 | First Hub Nights; claim wave; editorial spotlights |
| Weeks 7–12 | Sustain nights; deepen cohort; nurture waitlist toward MVP |
| MVP launch | Activate product journeys in messaging; shift CTA from waitlist → join/publish |
| Post-MVP | Retention content; open-call marketing (Phase 2); diaspora expansion |

### Contingency plans
| If… | Then… |
|---|---|
| Hub Night partner falls through | Rotate micro-venues / partner with existing nights; keep claim CTA |
| Low claim rate | In-person claim help; shorten profile minimum; DM follow-up within 48h |
| Audience > creators on waitlist | Bias outreach and CTAs to makers; gate founding benefits to creators |
| Message doesn’t land | A/B utility vs solidarity on landing; talk to 10 founders in cohort |
| Platform fatigue | Lead harder with IRL + “bring your IG/Behance work,” not “another app” |

### Budget (🔶 placeholders)
Bootstrapped. Prioritize: venue costs / Hub Night production, light design, domain/email tooling, optional micro-paid boosts for night posts. Defer brand video and large paid campaigns until name + voice are locked.

### Financial projections
No ROAS target in Phase 0 (pre-revenue). Success = seeded density + sustained nights + claim conversion. Phase 3 memberships/ticketing/commissions are out of scope for this plan’s ROI math until monetization decisions land.
